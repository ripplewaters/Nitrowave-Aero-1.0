NITROWAVE AERO — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Short version: Nitrowave Aero collects nothing about you. Everything it does
happens on your own computer.

THE APP

- No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no telemetry.
  Nitrowave sends no data about you or your listening to Nitrowave or anyone
  else.
- Settings (skin, visual world, volume, window size and position, the last
  YouTube link you typed) are stored locally in the app's WebView2 storage on
  your computer. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes them.
- Audio files you open are played directly from your disk. They are never
  uploaded or copied.
- System audio: to make the visuals react to music from other apps, Nitrowave
  reads the sound your computer is playing through Windows' loopback capture.
  It only computes loudness levels (bass, mids, treble) in memory, many times a
  second. Audio is never recorded, stored or transmitted, and the microphone is
  never used.
- Now playing: Nitrowave reads the title, artist, app name and playback
  position that Windows' media controls already show for the app you are
  playing music in (for example Spotify or a browser tab), and sends that app
  play, pause and skip commands when you press Nitrowave's buttons. This
  information is shown on screen only and is not stored or transmitted.
- YouTube links you paste open in your default web browser. From then on, your
  browser and YouTube handle playback under YouTube's and Google's privacy
  policies.
- Microsoft Edge WebView2, which the app uses to draw its window, is a Windows
  component maintained by Microsoft and governed by Microsoft's privacy terms.

THE WEBSITE

- The download site uses no cookies, analytics, trackers, embedded videos or
  third-party fonts or scripts.
- Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such
  as IP address, time and requested file) for security and operation, under the
  hosting provider's own retention policy.

YOUR RIGHTS

Because Nitrowave does not collect or hold personal data about you, there is
nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions, use the
contact details on the official website.

Changes to this policy will be published with new versions of the app and on
the website.
